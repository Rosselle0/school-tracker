# School

A board for the classes you still have. Open the site, sign in with Google, pick your school, and add classes by code or by dropping a picture or PDF.

Your university, classes, notes, and wallpaper sync to the Google account you sign in with, so the same account matches on your phone and computer. Someone else who signs in gets their own board. Settings can delete that tracker account without deleting the Google account.
